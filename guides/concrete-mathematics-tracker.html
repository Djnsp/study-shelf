Weekly rhythm (≈15 hrs): 6 hrs reading (two 3-hr sittings, pencil in hand — rederive every equation), 6 hrs exercises, 2 hrs reviewing/redoing what you missed, 1 hr playing: write tiny programs to test formulas on small cases.

Exercise policy (from the book's own preface): do every Warmup, at least half the Basics, and 2–3 Homework exercises per chapter. All answers are in Appendix A (p. 497) — check only after a serious attempt. Skip Exam/Bonus/Research problems on a first pass.

Rule of thumb: stuck on a section > 2 days → do the warmups anyway, mark it, move on, and revisit at the phase checkpoint. Momentum beats completeness on a first read.
